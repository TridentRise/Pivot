The thesis

Legal services are not discounted goods sold online. They're personal and complex — clients buy the firm and its expertise, and AI is critical but can only take you so far. AI handles the eighty percent of work that comes before the conversation. The team's job is to walk into more of the right rooms, not to spend hours hunting for them.

02 — Targeting

Define who you want to walk into a room with.

Set the ideal client profile once. AI hunts for matches automatically from then on.

Example ideal client profile

Ottawa-area founder, post-incorporation, pre-Series A

Stage Recently incorporated (0–24 months) or seed-stage
Sector SaaS, fintech, healthtech, AI
Geography Ottawa, Gatineau, Toronto corridor
Trigger Funding announcement, hiring spree, federal contract
Signal No GC, scaling team, contracts piling up

What the AI does with this profile, on its own

  1. Scans funding announcements, news, and public filings on a schedule.
  2. Builds a list of companies matching the criteria — name, founder, stage, signal.
  3. Enriches each entry with LinkedIn, website, and recent press.
  4. Pushes the list straight into the CRM as new lead cards, ready for the team.

03 — The flow

The automated funnel — until the moment it isn't.

AI runs the funnel until a meeting is booked. Then the lawyer takes over completely.

01
Find AI

Scheduled scan of funding databases, news, and public filings for companies matching the ICP. Runs weekly without anyone touching it.

02
Enrich & qualify AI

Pulls founder, LinkedIn, team size, recent press, and trigger events. Scores against ICP criteria. Discards anyone who already has counsel listed.

03
Track CRM

Each high-fit lead lands as a deal card with contact info, the trigger event, and a draft outreach email already attached.

04
The handoff Lawyer

Biz dev opens the CRM, reviews the draft, edits in 60 seconds, and sends it personally — in a human voice, with the lawyer's sign-off where it matters. The system never sends outreach on its own.

05
Connect & close Lawyer

Once a meeting is booked, the system steps back. Biz dev runs the discovery and builds the relationship; the lawyer scopes the work and closes the engagement — the part of the job no algorithm can do.

WHERE THE SYSTEM STOPS BEING A SYSTEM Biz dev fills the calendar with AI's help. Biz dev and the lawyer win the meetings together. That's the deal — and it's exactly why this works for a boutique firm.

06 — The division of labour

What AI runs. What only the lawyer can do.

A clear split — because boutique law is a relationship business, not a transactional one.

What AI runs

The volume work, before the conversation

  • Scan public sources for ICP-fit prospects
  • Monitor trigger events — funding, hires, expansions
  • Enrich each lead with structured context
  • Draft personalised outreach in the lawyer's voice
  • Track every deal, every email, every follow-up
  • Surface stalled deals and renewal opportunities
  • Run quietly, on a schedule, without prompting

What only humans can do

The relationship, once it begins

  • Personalise the first outreach before sending
  • Run the discovery call
  • Build trust with the founder
  • Scope bespoke work from a real conversation
  • Use judgment on fit, risk, and pricing
  • Earn referrals through reputation
  • Be the reason the client signs

The bet

If AI doubles the number of qualified meetings on the team's calendar this year, it doesn't matter that AI couldn't run the discovery or close the deal. The constraint on a boutique firm's growth has never been the lawyering — it's been getting in front of the right people. That's what this system solves.
